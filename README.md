## ADR Assessment

The reaction is suspected to be associated with amoxicillin because the
symptoms developed after initiation of the medication.

The generalized itching and skin rash are consistent with a possible
hypersensitivity reaction.

## Suspected ADR

- Suspected Drug: Amoxicillin
- Adverse Reaction: Generalized itching and skin rash
- Onset: 2 days after starting amoxicillin
- Dechallenge: Positive – symptoms resolved after discontinuation
- Outcome: Complete recovery

## Conclusion

Amoxicillin is the suspected medication associated with the reported
adverse drug reaction. The case should be considered a suspected ADR
and would require appropriate clinical evaluation and causality
assessment.
