## Reported symptoms

- Itching
- nausea
- Dizziness
## Suspected Drug
**Amoxicllin**
## Medication timeline
The patient started taking amoxicillin on Day 0.
The reported symptoms appeared approximately 5 days after starting the medication.
## ADR Assesment
The drug reaction is potentially drug-related because the symptoms developed after the initiation of amoxicillin.
Rash and itching may be consistent with a hypersensitivity reaction. Nausea can also occur with amoxicillin.
## Conclusion 
Amoxicillin is the suspected drug associated with te reported reaction.
This should be considered a suspected adverse drug reaction(ADR).
Further clinical information would be required for a complete causality assesment.
